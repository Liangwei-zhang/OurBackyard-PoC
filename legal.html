Privacy Policy

OurBackyard is designed with privacy as a core principle. This policy explains what data we collect, where it lives, and how it is protected.

1. Data We Collect

On your device only

  • Identity key — a cryptographic key pair generated locally, encrypted with your PIN
  • Item listings — title, description, photos, price, condition
  • Chat messages — encrypted end-to-end, stored in your local IndexedDB
  • Location — your approximate H3 geohash (≈5 km cell), used only for local discovery. Exact GPS coordinates are never stored or transmitted.

Shared with nearby peers (P2P)

  • Your peer ID (pseudonymous, derived from your public key)
  • Item listings you publish
  • Your H3 cell (neighborhood-level, not precise location)
  • Chat messages (encrypted so only the recipient can read them)

We do NOT collect

  • Your name, email, or phone number
  • Precise GPS location
  • Payment information
  • Browsing history or analytics

2. No Central Server

OurBackyard has no central database. Your data is not sent to or stored on our servers. Data flows directly between peers over encrypted WebRTC DataChannels and Nostr relays.

3. Location Data

We ask for location permission to determine your neighborhood H3 cell (approximately 5 km radius). This coarse location is used to connect you with nearby peers. We do not store or transmit your precise GPS coordinates. You will be shown a clear consent prompt before any location is used.

4. Encryption

  • All chat messages are encrypted end-to-end using ECDH + AES-GCM before leaving your device
  • Your private key is encrypted with AES-GCM derived from your PIN and never leaves your device in plain form
  • Connections use TLS/DTLS in transit

5. Third-Party Services

OurBackyard uses public Nostr relays for peer discovery and message routing. These relays may log metadata (event timestamps, relay routing). We use STUN/TURN servers for NAT traversal; these see your IP address during connection setup but not your message content.

6. Your Rights (PIPEDA)

Under Canada's PIPEDA, you have the right to:

  • Access — all your data is on your device; you can export it at any time
  • Correction — edit or delete your listings from within the app
  • Withdrawal — clear app data from your browser/device settings to remove all local data

Because we hold no central database, we cannot retrieve or delete data already propagated to other peers' devices.

7. Data Retention

Data lives on your device until you clear it. Published listings propagate to neighboring peers and may persist on their devices. Chat messages are stored locally on sender and recipient devices only.

8. Children

OurBackyard is not intended for users under 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
